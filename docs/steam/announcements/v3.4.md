## English

**Title:** v3.4 Update — Full Draw Pays Off

```
[h2]⚖️ Full draws now beat spam-clicking[/h2]
Tapping quickly used to out-damage fully charged shots. Fixed:
[list]
[*][b]Archer[/b] — a short rest after each shot, and damage now scales from 30% to 250% with draw
[*][b]Soul Sniper[/b] — damage now scales from 60% to 320% with charge
[/list]
Fully drawn and fully charged shots now deal the most damage per second.
```
