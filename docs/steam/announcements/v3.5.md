## English

**Title:** v3.5 Update — Character Abilities Reworked

```
[h2]🧙 32 brand-new special abilities[/h2]
Every special ability for the Swordsman, Rogue, Archer and Alchemist has been rebuilt from scratch. Each fits its character, and none overlap.
[list]
[*][b]Swordsman[/b] — War Hammer · Whip Sword · Knight's Lance / War Cry · Judgment Blade · Earth Splitter / Unyielding · Battle Fervor
[*][b]Rogue[/b] — Blowgun · Trick Cards · Razor Wire / Death Mark · Caltrops · Pickpocket / Evasion · Killing Spree
[*][b]Archer[/b] — Net Bow · Javelin · Burst Bow / Falcon Dive · Gale Arrow · Hunter's Focus / Trophy Hunter · Keep Distance
[*][b]Alchemist[/b] — Quicksilver Orb · Magnet Bomb · Firework Launcher / Philosopher's Stone · Rewind Elixir · Giant Potion / Alchemic Cycle · Volatile Research
[/list]
All 12 signature weapons also have new right-click ultimates.

[h2]🃏 Character level-up cards are now abilities[/h2]
Cards that only raised damage or range are gone. Each character gets four new ability cards instead (Shadow Clone, Blade Storm, Splitting Arrow, Homunculus and more).

[h2]⚖️ Balance[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Swordsman[/b] — 150% health, 300% basic attack damage, bigger longsword with a narrower swing
[*][b]Rogue[/b] — 70% health, Bleeding Dash distance 25, brief invincibility after the dash
[*][b]Archer[/b] — 50% health, 150% skill gauge, hold right-click to aim Arrow Rain
[/list]
```
