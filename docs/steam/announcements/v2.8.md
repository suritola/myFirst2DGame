## English

**Title:** v2.8 Update — Boss Intros · 6 New Boss Skills · Weapon Firing Effects

```
[h2]👑 Boss Intros[/h2]
When a boss first appears, the game pauses, the camera glides over to it, and the boss speaks — each with its own voice!
(Space: next line · ESC: skip)

[h2]🧟 New Boss Skills[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Lich King[/b]: Soul Chains · Grasp of the Dead
[*][b]Demon Lord[/b]: Hellfire Pillars · Lava Fissure
[*][b]King Slime[/b]: Acid Geysers · Slime Vortex
[/list]
All use brand-new pixel-art effects and are telegraphed before they hit. Screen shake now comes from just one skill per boss, and it's subtle.

[h2]🔫 Weapon Look & Firing Effects[/h2]
Your character now visibly holds the special weapon you're using, and every weapon has its own firing effect — recoil, sparks, shell casings, lightning and more.

[h2]📖 Story Controls[/h2]
Space now advances dialogue (finishing the current line instantly if it's still typing). Press ESC to skip the whole scene.
```
