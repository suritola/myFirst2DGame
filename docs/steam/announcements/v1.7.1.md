## English

**Title:** 1.7.1 Update — Calmer Notifications

```
[h2]🪧 Smaller, calmer notifications[/h2]
Shop and mid-boss alerts and the "Level Up!" banner now appear small at the top of the screen instead of covering the middle.

[h2]👹 Mid-boss marker[/h2]
The gold glow is gone; only a small name tag above the health bar remains.

※ Version numbers use a new format from now on. (v3.9 → 1.7.1)
```
