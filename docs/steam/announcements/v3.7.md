## English

**Title:** v3.7 Update — Faster Pace, Shorter and Spicier Boss Fights

```
[h2]⏩ About 30% faster pace[/h2]
Fewer kills are needed to reach each boss, and enemies spawn faster. Target clear times: about 20 min on Easy, 25 on Normal, 30 on Hard. You get more XP and coins per kill to make up for it.

[h2]👑 Bosses: less health, harder hits[/h2]
[list]
[*]All bosses have much less health and go down in 1–3 minutes
[*]In exchange, boss skills and attacks hit 25% harder and come more often
[/list]

[h2]Other changes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Invincibility after being hit: 0.8s → 0.4s
[*]Fixed the "Level Up!" banner covering the shop
[*]Doubled the distance for opening a shop stall with Space
[/list]
```
